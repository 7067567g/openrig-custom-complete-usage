Service-level memory limits and global/shared-scope guidance.

## Added

- `examples/memory-limits/agent-host-memory.conf`
- `examples/memory-limits/app-service-memory.conf`
- Long-running service section in `docs/MEMORY-SAFETY.md` and Chinese counterpart.

## Policy

```text
agent host:    MemoryHigh=2500M  MemoryMax=4G    MemorySwapMax=1G
app service:   MemoryHigh=512M   MemoryMax=1G    MemorySwapMax=256M
```

These are user-level global service limits. Per-run seats still need their own
`systemd-run --scope` limits.
